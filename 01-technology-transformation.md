# Technology Transformation / Modernization

## Framework

1. **Current state:** The client's people, processes, and technology today, and what they cost.
2. **Target state:** The people, processes, and technology the client needs to meet its business goals.
3. **Transition options:** The possible paths from current to target, compared on cost, value, and risk.
4. **Execution:** How the chosen path is phased, delivered, adopted, and measured.
