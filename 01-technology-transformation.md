# Technology Transformation / Modernization

**Status:** Changed

**Issue:** "Business case" overlapped with evaluating the options, since both weigh cost against value.

## Framework

1. **Current state:** What systems, processes, and costs exist today.
2. **Target state:** What the client needs to look like in the future.
3. **Transition options:** The possible paths from current to target, compared on cost, value, and risk.
4. **Execution:** How the chosen path is phased, delivered, and measured.
