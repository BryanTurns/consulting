Work You’ll Do
As a Technology Transformation – Packages, Platforms, & AI Analyst, you’ll join a dynamic team helping clients solve complex business challenges through enterprise technology, data, and AI-enabled solutions. In this role, you will help guide clients through their technology transformation journeys by leveraging cutting edge technology and AI-enabled improve efficiency, increase agility, and create lasting business impact. 

Your work will include: 

    Automating business processes with AI and intelligent workflow solutions to drive efficiency and innovation
    Using AI tools and agents to help assess current state processes, design future state models, and identify gaps
    Supporting the design and agentification of workflows to improve efficiency and scalability 
    Modernizing legacy technology systems to enhance agility and performance
    Implementing enterprise-wide cloud and application transformations for secure, resilient operations
    Deploying advanced data analytics platforms that enable actionable insights and smarter decision making
    Integrating digital tools seamlessly to enable unified, future-ready ecosystems
    Delivering end-to-end technology transformation initiatives to ensure lasting impact

Key Responsibilities

    Support clients in transforming business processes through agentic AI and enterprise technology solutions
    Design, develop, and implement technology solutions across packages and platforms such as SAP, Oracle, Salesforce, Workday, Anaplan, and ServiceNow
    Use AI tools to build prompts, skills and workflow enhancements that improve process design and execution 
    Conduct current state assessments, future state process design, gap analysis, workflow optimization, testing, and performance measurement
    Collaborate with cross-functional teams to architect and deliver innovative, client-centric technology solutions
    Leverage data-driven insights and technical expertise to enable successful, sustainable business transformation
    Drive user adoption and change management, ensuring technology investments align with client business objectives
