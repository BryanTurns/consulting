# Market Entry / Growth

**Status:** Changed

**Issue:** "Risks" cut across every other bucket, so risks now get raised within each one.

## Framework

1. **Market:** How big, fast-growing, and profitable the market is.
2. **Competition:** Who's already there and how they'd respond.
3. **Client capabilities:** Whether the client has what it takes to win.
4. **Entry economics:** What it costs to enter, including build vs. buy vs. partner, and when it pays back.
