# Operations / Process Improvement

**Status:** Changed

**Issue:** Time, capacity, quality, and cost are linked metrics, not separate causes; for example, rework drives both time and cost. They now serve as the yardstick for measuring each bucket below.

## Framework

1. **Demand:** How much work comes in, and how variable it is.
2. **Capacity:** How much people and tools can handle.
3. **Process:** How work flows between steps, including handoffs, waiting, and rework.
