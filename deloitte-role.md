# Role: Technology Transformation – Packages, Platforms, & AI Analyst

## Summary

- **Firm:** Deloitte Consulting (US)
- **Level:** Entry-level Analyst
- **Practice:** Technology Transformation, which helps clients solve business problems with enterprise software, data, and AI
- **Core idea:** Bridge business and technology, and turn technology investments into measurable business value

## What the Work Covers

1. **AI and automation:** Automating business processes with AI, agents, and intelligent workflows. This includes "agentifying" workflows, meaning redesigning them so AI agents perform steps.
2. **Process design:** Assessing current-state processes, designing future-state models, and identifying the gaps between them, often using AI tools to help.
3. **Legacy modernization:** Replacing or upgrading old systems to improve agility and performance.
4. **Cloud and application transformation:** Enterprise-wide moves to the cloud and new applications, with a focus on security and resilience.
5. **Data and analytics platforms:** Deploying platforms that turn data into insights and better decisions.
6. **Integration:** Connecting digital tools into one unified ecosystem.
7. **End-to-end delivery:** Running transformation programs from assessment through implementation to lasting impact.

## Platforms Named

SAP, Oracle, Salesforce, Workday, Anaplan, ServiceNow

## Day-to-Day Responsibilities

- Run current-state assessments, future-state design, gap analysis, workflow optimization, testing, and performance measurement
- Design, build, and implement solutions on the platforms above
- Use AI tools to write prompts, skills, and workflow enhancements that improve process design and execution
- Work with cross-functional teams to design and deliver solutions
- Use data to guide and justify transformation decisions
- Drive user adoption and change management so technology spending matches the client's business goals

## What They Look For

- Curious, collaborative, and analytical
- Able to talk to both business and technical stakeholders
- Comfortable with ambiguity and quick to learn
- Uses AI and data to solve problems
- Proactive problem solver and clear communicator

## How Deloitte Frames Its Consulting

Solutions are built within the client's operating model, accounting for four elements: **people, intellectual capital, technology, and processes.**
