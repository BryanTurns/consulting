# Platform / Vendor Selection

## Framework

1. **Fit:** How well the platform meets the client's requirements, including integration with existing systems.
2. **Vendor:** Whether the company behind it is stable, has a strong product roadmap, and provides good support.
3. **Total cost:** What the client pays over the platform's life, including licensing, implementation, and running costs.
4. **Transition:** How long and disruptive the move would be, and whether the client's people can adopt it.

Define the client's requirements first, then score each option against them. Options include staying on the current system or building custom.
